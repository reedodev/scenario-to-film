# Generation and cost

Paid generation starts only after gate 2 (cost approved). This skill never adds permission to pay
or subscribe.

## Job safety

- Before pressing generate, confirm the model, length, resolution, number of takes and uploaded
  images on screen. A cost-preview click has once started a real job.
- If a request times out or the page shows no result, **do not resend the unchanged request.**
  Open the job list, find whether the job exists, then wait, fetch it or cancel it. A resend is
  a second charge.
- A shot that fails twice is simplified or cut.
- Generate one shot at a time and check it (identity, space, eye line, action) before the next.

## Cost log

For each shot record: model, resolution, seconds, takes, credits used, credits left, whether the
number is measured from the account or estimated. Do not mix estimates with measured values;
unknown costs are written as unknown. Fewer takes, a shorter clip or a lower resolution is not a
saving unless the check result is the same.

## Reuse

Reuse a reference still or a previs clip only if its inputs (character words, wardrobe, location,
camera) are unchanged. Anything whose input changed is rebuilt. Anything unchanged is reused to
save credits.

## Compare a change fairly

When changing a model, prompt or setting, compare before and after on the same shot with the same
reference image and record credits, seconds and the result. Comparing different shots proves
nothing. Say "not compared" if you did not.

## Contest and platform rules

Read the rules before generating: allowed tools, whether outside images or footage are allowed,
length, ratio, required text, AI label. Unclear rules go into `unknowns.md`; do not guess.

## Check each clip, then check the neighbours

After each clip, make a frame sheet (one frame per second) and look at it before you generate the
next. Check against the state table in the shot list: same face, same clothes, props where the
story left them. Then, before assembly, view the clips that will sit next to each other in one
sheet. A mistake that is only visible between two clips (a prop that came back) cannot be seen in
either clip alone.

## Before you press generate

- Confirm the account you are logged in as. A restarted or new browser session can be logged out
  or logged in as another account, and credits belong to an account.
- Confirm visibility and privacy settings. Some services publish what you generate by default, and
  some programs only count public clips. Decide on purpose, before generating, because the
  setting applies at generation time.
- Read the clip length limits in the service's own screen (the first film's service had a 5 s
  minimum; shorter shots were trimmed in the edit).
- Do not click around the service's pricing or upgrade pages while looking for a price. Stray
  clicks opened a checkout page once (nothing was paid). Read the price on the generate screen.
- Long generations can outlast one tool call. Wait in short steps and look at the job list; do not
  resend while waiting.
